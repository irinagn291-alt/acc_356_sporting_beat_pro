Create soundtracks for 16 animated scenes across soccer, tennis, basketball, and track cycling. Try ten virtual props at the Foley desk, then tap, swipe, hold, or drag to match what happens on screen.

Follow the director’s cues across the Contact, Movement, and Ambience tracks. Save a passing standard take to unlock the next order, or choose Creative take to hide the cues and perform the scene your way.

Watch your take in Screening and check the feedback for each cue. Use Timeline to select a track and re-record a section while keeping the rest of your performance. Save multiple versions and replay them from the Tape shelf.

Complete all 16 orders and choose one saved take from each sport to unlock Final broadcast. It plays all four selections in a single program.

Play offline on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, in portrait or landscape.
