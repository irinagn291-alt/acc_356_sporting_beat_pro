Create soundtracks for 16 animated scenes across football, tennis, basketball and track cycling. At the Foley desk, try ten virtual props and use taps, swipes, holds and drags to match the action on screen.

Follow the director’s cues across Contact, Movement and Ambience. Save a passing standard take to unlock the next order, or choose Creative take to perform without cue guides and save your own interpretation.

Replay your take in Screening and see which cues landed early, late or on time. In Timeline, pick a track and re-record just the section you want to fix. Save multiple versions and revisit them on the Tape shelf.

Complete all 16 orders and select one saved take from each sport to unlock Final broadcast, which plays your four selections as one programme.

Play offline on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, in portrait or landscape.
